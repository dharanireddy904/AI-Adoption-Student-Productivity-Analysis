# AI Adoption & Student Productivity Analysis

## Project Overview

This Power BI dashboard analyzes AI adoption patterns among students using survey data collected through Google Forms. The project explores AI usage behavior, productivity impact, satisfaction levels, trust perceptions, concerns regarding AI, and future outlook.

The dashboard was designed to identify relationships between AI usage habits and student productivity, satisfaction, and trust perceptions through interactive visual analytics.

## Dataset

- 55 student survey responses
- Data collected using Google Forms
- Cleaned and transformed using Excel and Power BI
- Fields include AI tool usage, study year, field of study, productivity rating, satisfaction level, trust level, and future AI perceptions

## Tools Used

- Power BI
- Microsoft Excel
- Power Query
- DAX

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. AI Adoption Overview
Provides an overview of AI usage patterns, preferred AI tools, usage duration, frequency, and respondent demographics.

### 2. Productivity & User Behaviour
Analyzes the relationship between AI usage, productivity levels, satisfaction ratings, and the most valued AI features.

### 3. Trust & Future Outlook
Examines trust levels, key concerns regarding AI, and student perceptions of AI’s future role in education and work.

### 4. Executive Summary
Summarizes major findings, insights, and recommendations derived from the analysis.

## Key Insights

- ChatGPT was the most widely used AI platform (~58% of usage).
- Personalized assistance emerged as the most valued AI capability.
- Students who used AI more frequently reported higher productivity levels.
- Average satisfaction with AI tools was positive (3.71/5).
- Privacy concerns were the most significant barrier to adoption.
- More than 65% of respondents believe AI will become essential in education and future careers.
